About MindScore AI

MindScore AI is a machine-learning project designed to explore how lifestyle, academic habits, social-media usage, sleep, physical activity, and stress-related factors can be used to estimate a mental health score.

The system uses a trained machine-learning regression model exposed through a FastAPI backend.
